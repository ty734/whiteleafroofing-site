Repair or replacement

How I call it on a Chandler roof

The question behind almost every roof repair in Chandler AZ is the same one: is this a fix, or is this the start of a new roof? Here's the honest version of how I decide, so you can see where your roof probably lands before I ever get on a ladder.

What's wrong What the fix actually involves My call
A few cracked or slipped tiles Reset or swap the affected tiles and check the underlayment beneath them while we're up there. Your existing tiles can usually be reused or matched. Repair. Almost always the cheapest call on the board.
Leak at a vent, chimney or valley Reseal or rebuild the flashing at that penetration. Most leaks I chase in Chandler start here, not out in the open field of the roof. Repair. Fix the detail, not the roof.
One failed section of underlayment Lift the tiles over that section, replace the felt, relay the tiles. The rest of the roof is left alone. Repair. No reason to touch sound areas.
Monsoon or haboob wind damage Full walk-around, an honest written damage report, then repairs to whatever actually moved. If it warrants a claim, I document it for your adjuster. Usually repair. Scope depends on what the storm found.
Underlayment failing across the whole roof Lift all the tiles, replace all the underlayment, relay your same tiles. This is a re-felt and relay, not a brand new roof, and it costs far less than one. Bigger job. Still not a full replacement.
Rot in the decking, or repairs stacking up yearly Once wood is going and you're paying for a repair every monsoon, you're renting a roof. I'll show you the numbers side by side and let you decide. Replacement. This is where the repair math stops working.

What homeowners ask me

How much does roof repair cost in Chandler, AZ?

Most repairs run $500 to $3,500 depending on what's wrong and how much material is involved. Anything past about $3,500 usually means a replacement makes more sense, and I'll show you exactly why before you spend a dime. Every job gets a free inspection and an individual bid.

How fast can you get to a roof leak in Chandler?

We're based right here in Chandler at Gilbert Rd and Queen Creek Rd, so I can usually get to an active leak the same or next day. Leaks and fresh storm damage go to the front of the line.

Can you fix a tile roof without replacing the whole thing?

Very often, yes. Slipped or cracked tiles can be reset or swapped, and a small section of failed underlayment can be repaired on its own. If the underlayment is failing across the whole roof, we lift your tiles, re-felt, and relay the same tiles instead of selling you a brand new roof.

Will you try to talk me into a full replacement?

No. Repairs come first at White Leaf. I only bring up replacement when the repair math stops making sense for you, and when that happens I'll walk you through the numbers so you can see it yourself.

Can a monsoon damage my roof without any visible leak?

Yes. Monsoon wind can lift tiles, shift flashing, and tear underlayment without a single drip showing up inside. That's why I do free walk-around inspections after big storms, so small damage gets caught before the next one turns it into water in your ceiling.

Do I need a permit for a roof repair in Chandler, AZ?

Small like-for-like repairs often don't require one, but the threshold is set by the city, and replacement work does require a permit. When I bid a job, the bid states whether a permit applies, and if there's any doubt I ask the city so you don't have to. Permits and inspections are my paperwork, not yours. The full rundown is in my guide to roofing permits and codes in Chandler.

My Chandler roof is only 15 years old. Why does it need repair already?

Because on a tile roof it usually isn't the tiles failing, it's the underlayment beneath them. Concrete tiles last decades; the felt under them is the actual waterproof layer, and it wears out much sooner in Arizona sun. Most Chandler neighborhoods built in the 1990s and 2000s are hitting that window right now, which is why so much of my Chandler work is lifting the tiles, replacing the underlayment, and relaying the same tiles. More on the timeline in how long a tile roof lasts in Arizona.

Do you repair flat and foam roofs in Chandler?

Yes. Flat and low-slope sections are common on Chandler homes, often over a patio, addition, or carport. Repairs there usually mean addressing ponding areas, failed seams, scuppers and drains, or a worn elastomeric coating. Foam roofs get recoated rather than torn off when the foam underneath is still sound.
