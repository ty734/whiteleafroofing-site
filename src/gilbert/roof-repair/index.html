Price and the repair math

Roof repair cost in Gilbert, AZ, and when it stops making sense

Most roof repair in Gilbert AZ lands between $500 and $3,500. Smaller jobs sit around $500 to $1,200; bigger ones run $1,200 to $3,500. Where yours falls depends on what failed and how far the water traveled, and I can't tell you that honestly until I've been on the roof. That's why the inspection is free and the bid is individual.

Past about $3,500, the conversation changes. Here's how I sort what I find on a Gilbert roof into fix it, or start thinking about the whole thing.

What you're seeing What it usually turns out to be Repair or replace?
Tiles in the yard after a windy night Wind lifted or broke a handful of tiles. The felt underneath is now exposed to sun and the next rain, so I check it while the tiles are off. Repair. Reset or match the tiles, patch any felt that got hit.
A water stain that grows after every rain Usually a flashing detail: a valley, a vent stack, a wall where the roof meets stucco. The stain is rarely right under the leak, because water travels along the felt first. Repair. Trace it to the source and rebuild that detail.
A leak in one area of a tile roof that's 20+ years old A section of underlayment has given out. On Gilbert homes from the late 1990s and 2000s this is the call I get most. Repair the section. Lift the tiles there, new felt, relay.
Leaks showing up in several rooms The felt is failing across most of the roof, not in one spot. The tiles themselves are usually fine. Re-felt and relay. New underlayment, same tiles. Bigger than a repair, far less than a new roof.
Ponding or cracked coating on a flat or foam section Failed seams, a blocked scupper, or a coating that's past its 5 to 7 year life. Repair and recoat while the foam underneath is still sound.
Soft spots underfoot, or a repair bill every monsoon Water has reached the decking, or the roof is past the point where patches hold. Replacement. I'll show you both numbers side by side.

How much does roof repair cost in Gilbert, AZ?

Most Gilbert repairs land between $500 and $3,500. The exact number depends on what failed and how far the water got, so I inspect first, free, then bid the job individually. Once a repair pushes past about $3,500, a replacement usually makes more sense, and I'll be upfront about that.

Do you handle emergency roof repairs in Gilbert?

Yes. Active leaks and fresh storm damage jump the line. Our shop is on Gilbert Rd at the Chandler border, so getting to any Gilbert neighborhood quickly is easy, often the same or next day.

My tile roof is leaking. Do I need a whole new roof?

Usually not. Most Gilbert tile leaks come from a failed patch of underlayment or a slipped tile, and both can be repaired on their own. Even when the underlayment is done across the whole roof, your tiles can usually be lifted and relaid over new felt, which costs far less than a new roof.

How do I know if monsoon wind damaged my roof?

Often you won't until it rains again, because wind lifts tiles and flashing without leaving an obvious leak. If a storm rolled through your part of Gilbert, take a photo of anything that looks off and text it to me at 480-363-2898, or just have me do a free walk-around.

Why call White Leaf instead of one of the big roofing companies?

Because you get the owner, not a commissioned salesman. I run a lean, hand-picked crew, so I'm almost always able to beat the big companies' bids, and 90% of my work comes from referrals, which only happens when people get treated honestly.

Will homeowners insurance pay for storm damage roof repair in Gilbert?

Sometimes. It depends on your policy and on whether the damage came from the storm or from age. What I can do is put the facts on paper: photos, what moved, what it takes to fix it. If the damage justifies a claim, I help you through it. If it doesn't, I'll tell you that too, and the repair is usually small enough that you're better off just fixing it.

What is a re-felt and relay, and when does a Gilbert roof need one?

It's when the felt under your tiles has failed across most of the roof but the tiles are still good. I lift the tiles, replace all the underlayment, and relay your same tiles. It's a bigger job than a spot repair and far less than a new roof. A lot of Gilbert tile roofs built in the late 1990s and 2000s are reaching that point now.

Do you repair flat and foam roofs in Gilbert?

Yes. Patio covers, additions and low-slope sections leak at seams, scuppers and spots where water ponds. Foam roofs get repaired where they're damaged and recoated every 5 to 7 years, which is a lot cheaper than tearing them off while the foam underneath is still sound.

Are you going to try to sell me a new roof?

No. If a repair fixes it, a repair is what you'll get from me. I only bring up replacement when repairs keep stacking up or the decking is going, and then I put the repair math and the replacement number side by side so you can make the call yourself.
